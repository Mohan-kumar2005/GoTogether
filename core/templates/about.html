HOW IT STARTED

Why We Created GoTogether

During Pongal, Diwali, Eid, and long weekends, many students and professionals travel from cities like Chennai, Bengaluru, and Coimbatore to their hometowns.

At the same time, bus fares can become expensive and people travelling alone often bear the full cost of fuel. Meanwhile, cars and bikes travel along the same routes with available passenger space.

We imagined a simpler solution: help people discover suitable travel companions, coordinate their journeys, and share agreed travel expenses.

Share the Ride, Share the Journey. Make every trip count.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our Core Principles

The values that guide the GoTogether experience.

Safety First

Encourage responsible travel, appropriate verification, valid driving licences, and compliance with road safety requirements.

Fair Cost Sharing

Make agreed travel expenses clear to everyone before a journey begins.

Community Respect

Encourage clear communication, punctual pickups, courtesy, and respect between travel companions.
